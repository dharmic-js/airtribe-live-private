    How it started

    We started with a belief: learning should be live, hands-on, and community-driven,
    not passive content consumption. Our programs are designed for people who want
    to build solutions to real problems, not just watch lectures.


    Led by practitioners from leading technology companies, our programs have helped
    1 lakh+ professionals grow into more confident builders. These programs help you
    develop real-world judgment and leverage with AI to build stronger careers as roles continue to evolve.
