Questions

Asked in every meeting, answered here

What have you actually done for other companies?

Six enterprise engagements delivered or in delivery, across creative, HR, commercial and engineering teams. The case studies page has the numbers and full stories.

Which AI tools will you train us on?

The ones that fit your work and your IT policy - chosen at discovery, not before. We teach across the modern stack: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, the automation platforms and the full creative stack - 25+ tools in all.

Our data cannot leave our environment. Can this still work?

Yes. Deployments are designed around India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) from day one: data stays inside your environment, is never used for model training, and leadership keeps full admin control and monitoring. We've run a complete program even under a European parent's stricter GDPR-aligned governance. Data security is designed in at proposal stage, not bolted on.

Our IT environment is restricted. Can this still work?

Yes - we've delivered a complete program 100% inside a client's Microsoft ecosystem: no third-party APIs, no external hosting, nothing requiring admin or developer access. The curriculum adapts to your constraints, not the other way around.

Is this only for technical teams?

No. Most of our corporate learners are non-technical - HR, commercial, ops, finance, audit, creative, client services. Directors and CFOs in our current cohorts build live dashboards and agents without writing code. If they can write an email or fill an Excel sheet, they can do this.

How customised is the curriculum, really?

Every program starts with discovery - often on-site - and the syllabus is assembled from our tracks around what we find. Session examples use your briefs, templates and data formats. No two client programs we've delivered have had the same session plan.

What happens after the program ends?

Your team keeps everything it built: prompt libraries, Skills, agents, automations, dashboards, pipelines - owned permanently by you, nothing licensed back. Depending on the engagement, mentor support continues for up to a year (including one-on-one mentors per participant), and all participants join Airtribe's 7,000+ member practitioner community.

Do you provide the AI tool licenses?

We can. Recent engagements have bundled ChatGPT Premium, Claude Pro and Microsoft Copilot Premium into the program cost so learners build from Session 1 without waiting on procurement.

How is it delivered and scheduled?

Live online cohort sessions (recorded, on the Airtribe LMS), scheduled around your team - weekday evenings for teams that work weekends, fixed weekly slots for manufacturing shifts. Offline workshops available in major cities. Attendance and progress reports go to leadership after every session.
