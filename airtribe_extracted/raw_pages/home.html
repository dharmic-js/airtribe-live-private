Founders' Note

To every builder reading this

We've both been the person wondering if it was already too late to start over.

The ambition was never the problem. What we didn't have was a clear path, someone a few steps ahead to ask, and a room full of people building the same thing at the same time. We got there eventually, slower and lonelier than we needed to.

Airtribe is the thing we wish we'd had. Mentors who've actually built what they teach. Work that ends in something you shipped. And a tribe that stays long after the cohort does.

Careers are built, not handed out. Start with one project. We'll be right here.

Navneet Sharma and Dhaval Trivedi
